# Verifiable AI-assisted literature work

Doctoral research pilot (management sciences) on the verifiability of AI-agent workflows in academic literature work.

## Research question

When AI agents take part in source discovery, literature and author mapping, and evidence extraction, how far can their outputs be traced to verifiable sources, and at what human verification cost?

## Design (exploratory)

- Bounded task units run under matched conditions.
- Single-agent and coordinated configurations are compared.
- Initial assessments stay independent before any comparison.
- Every assessable output is checked by the researcher against its sources, using a rubric fixed before production runs.

Primary outcome: the share of outputs supported by verifiable evidence. Secondary outcomes: unsupported claims, incorrect references, human verification time, rework and interruptions.

## Status

The pilot is in preparation. No results are available yet. This repository will host the non-confidential instruments once they are validated: the evaluation rubric, the run-logging templates and the verification protocol. Field data, internal instructions and detailed search strategies will not be published.
